# BUYUR Management Panel

> A growth and sales operations panel that connects campaigns, leads, pipeline work, demos, deals, customers, and internal project tasks in one workspace.

## What it does

BUYUR Management Panel gives teams a focused operating view for marketing and sales work. It is built around the flow from campaign to lead, lead to pipeline, pipeline to demo, demo to deal, and deal to customer. The project also includes project-management views for team execution.
